# Martin Lukas Ostachowski (MLO) — reference notes

Compiled from sovrn.art, which published *Noctilucent Mementi*. Companion to

## The artist

Known as **"the cloud artist."** He began tokenizing his work in **June 2018**
and became a formative figure in cryptoart, making fundamental contributions to
the cultural understanding of the medium. His early work uses geometric
minimalism and artistic data visualization to investigate and teach what
blockchains and tokens might mean.

With *Cloud Seed Phrase Visualization* (September 2018) he arrived at a way of
translating data into metaphor that has been the spine of his practice since.

Born in Poland, raised in Germany, emigrated to Canada; he has lived in more than
33 places. He also maintains a **definitive History of Crypto Art timeline**,
which is a reference in its own right:
<https://mlo.art/research/history-of-crypto-art/>

In his words: "I find the analogy to clouds particularly intriguing, as we store
only the rights to assets in our digital wallets while the actual digital assets
remain permanently on the blockchain; the digital assets therefore remain
intangible much like clouds in the sky."

- <https://mlo.art>

## The work

| | |
|---|---|
| Title | *Noctilucent Mementi* |
| Released | 20 October 2023 |
| Works | 210 |
| Chain | Ethereum |
| Contract | `0x2c85dAf0e2a6f040736fdA46fc3c6676223d2A42` |

A generative chronicle of **five years** of MLO's travels and cloud work in
cryptoart, 2018–2023, across **4 continents and 11 countries**. Each work is a
location-specific memento, and together they form a visual timeline of the
artist's development, milestones and movement.

*Noctilucent* means luminous in the dark, and refers to high, normally invisible
clouds. *Mementi* are his curated visual memories. His own line for it:
**"Noctilucent clouds are invisible memories, glowing when revisited."**

It is his first serial collection, released to mark five years since his first
cloud artwork in September 2018.

The count is `totalSupply()` read from the contract. Note it is **210**, which is
not a number the page leads with.

## Provenance

**Francisco Carolinum, Linz — permanent collection.** *Noctilucent Memento #142*
is one of eleven works in the Sovrn Full Ethereum Set. Curator Julia Staudach; OÖ
Landes-Kultur GmbH. <https://www.sovrn.art/collections/fransisco-carolinum>

**ART OF PUNK** — Francisco Carolinum, Linz, and the metaverse Voxels; OÖ
Landes-Kultur GmbH, 4 September 2024 – 26 January 2025, curated by Julia
Staudach. Billed as the world's first museum exhibition of artists from the
CryptoPunk community — only artists who own one or more CryptoPunks. MLO was
among them, alongside Pindar Van Arman. Same venue and curator as the Sovrn Full
Ethereum Set, but a separate exhibition.

*This one is not documented on sovrn.art* — it comes from our submission corpus,
so here is the primary source rather than our word for it:
<https://www.ooekultur.at/en/exhibitions-events/art-of-punk> — **note this
  page has since been taken down** (404 as of 27 August 2026); the museum itself
  is at <https://www.ooekultur.at/en/location-detail/francisco-carolinum-linz>
