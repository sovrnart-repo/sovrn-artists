# Look Highward — reference notes

Compiled from sovrn.art, which published *Possibility Spaces*. Companion to

## The artist

Look Highward works at enormous scale, through an emergent series of **calls and
responses** between artist and AI. The pieces are deep-zoomable images, often
over **250 million pixels**.

## Display note: scale is the medium

This is the one Sovrn collection where the file size is doing artistic work
rather than being an artefact of production. A piece is **21,000 × 12,000
pixels** — over 250 million — and is made to be **zoomed into**, not merely
looked at. The realms hold detail that only appears when you go in.

Two consequences for a marketplace page:

- A single fixed-size preview loses the work. If you can offer a zoom or
  deep-zoom viewer for any collection, this is the one that earns it.
- Do not judge these by how they read as a thumbnail. A thumbnail of a
  250-megapixel piece is a summary of a summary.

Our own pages present them large and full-bleed for this reason.
