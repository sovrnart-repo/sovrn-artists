# bashobits — reference notes

Compiled from sovrn.art, which published *Seasons of Mobility*. Companion to
**raster.art/artist/bashobits/about**.

**The most important thing in this document is the work count.** It is not
twelve. See the handling note.

---

## The artist

"Trained by a mix of humans, AI systems, and spirits — an artist in motion,
imperfectly." Named for the poet **Matsuo Bashō**.

They have extended the Mobility world well beyond the Sovrn release, with
*Freedom of Mobility*, *Memories of Mobility* and *Ghosts of Mobility*, the game
*Lost Mobility: Sand Wave Drift*, and the separate collection *Berry Based*. They
have also collaborated with Pindar Van Arman as part of the **bitGAN Collabs**.

- <https://bashobits.com>

## The work

| | |
|---|---|
| Title | *Seasons of Mobility* |
| Released | 15 April 2023 |
| Works | **365** |
| Chain | Ethereum |
| Contract | `0xAF116C78180F6b5dc2cFC064A2e5ae2f99505d8F` |

It is the year **1010 AH — After Humanity**. We are long gone, but the autonomous
trains, buses and cars we built have become a new species that calls itself
Mobility.

The work was made in the **wabi-sabi** tradition, which finds beauty in the
imperfect, the impermanent and the incomplete. The colours and scenes turn
through the four seasons across a full year, and each piece shows everyday life
in a future that is both familiar and surreal.

---

## Handling note: 365, not twelve

Our own page describes "twelve works, three to a season, turning through a full
year." **That is the display selection on the page, not the size of the
collection.** The contract holds **365** — one for each day of the year, which is
what the seasonal conceit is actually built on.

We know this is easy to get wrong because we got it wrong ourselves: an earlier
pass read the number out of the page heading and published it as the collection
size. The figure above is `totalSupply()` read from the contract.

If a page states a number inside a display heading, check the contract.

## Provenance

**Francisco Carolinum, Linz — permanent collection.** *Admiral Rush* is one of
eleven works in the Sovrn Full Ethereum Set. Curator Julia Staudach; OÖ
Landes-Kultur GmbH. <https://www.sovrn.art/museums/fransisco-carolinum>

bashobits wrote an essay placing *Admiral Rush* in the art history of mobility,
from Turner onward. Ask us if you would like it — it is in our corpus rather than
on the site.

**NEORT · Screens Contextualized — Shibuya, Tokyo.** *Weather Writes*, February
2026. *Aura Afterglow*, a custom combination of three pieces from *Freedom of
Mobility*, was selected and shown across **58 digital signage screens** in
Shibuya. *This is not documented on sovrn.art* — source:
<https://screens.neort.io>

Note that the NEORT piece draws on *Freedom of Mobility*, not on the Sovrn
release, so it belongs in the artist's wider history rather than under *Seasons
of Mobility*.
