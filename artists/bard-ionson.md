# Bård Ionson — reference notes

Compiled from sovrn.art, which published *Painting with Fire*. Companion to
**raster.art/artist/bard-ionson/about**.

---

## The artist

A pioneer of crypto and generative art whose GAN-algorithms flesh out small
universes across image and written word. His work is held in the **Oxford
Bodleian Weston Library** and the **Centre Pompidou** collections.

- <https://bardionson.com>

## The work

| | |
|---|---|
| Title | *Painting with Fire: a history in GANs* |
| Released | 20 December 2023 |
| Works | 200 |
| Chain | Ethereum |
| Contract | `0x695bcE11aBe0ec88e83Ad67a0e7e64d21E26F71A` |

The evolution of GANs from **2014 to 2023**, seen through the image of fire, made
using **14 vintage GAN models**.

Ionson articulates the emergence of synthetic image generation and the strange
turns it took over that decade. Each of the 200 works invokes fire, showing the
wild variety of ways early generative visual algorithms imagined this elemental
force — fire as the quintessence of human transformation, and as the thing the
models were themselves being reshaped by.

The count is `totalSupply()` read from the contract.

---

## The trait that makes this collection unusual

Each of the 200 works carries a **"Historic Event" trait linking it to one entry
on Ionson's GAN timeline.** The collection is therefore a chronology as well as a
series: the traits are documentary rather than decorative, and the set read in
order is a history of the technique.

If you surface traits, this one is worth surfacing prominently — it is the
organising idea of the work rather than an attribute of an individual piece.
Ionson's own long account, including the full GAN timeline, sits in our corpus;
ask and we will send it.
