# Norman Harman — reference notes

Compiled from sovrn.art, which published both collections. Companion to
**raster.art/artist/norman-harman/about**.

---

## The artist

Norman Harman ("Norrie", b. 1977) is a Scottish painter and a member of **Ltd Ink
Corporation**. Edinburgh College of Art, 2001; winner of the Royal Scottish
Academy's **Latimer Prize**.

He corrupts digital files with a purpose-built algorithm to reveal what he calls
"the beauty of lost transmissions" — work often described as **exploded
paintings**. He mixes painting, glitch and AI rather than working in any one of
them.

## The two works

| | *SIGHTSEERS* | *Sightseers: Perimeter Town* |
|---|---|---|
| Released | 1 October 2022 | 3 June 2023 |
| Works | 500 | 250 |
| Chain | Ethereum | Ethereum |
| Contract | `0xDf4464AC5c4346B8FF1501E3AE2790C4AE3E610a` | `0xaa4bc994775a0d19Ff1c01310191Df6521af12dD` |

Both counts are `totalSupply()` read from the contracts.

***SIGHTSEERS*** is Harman's first serial collection and a defining work of the
early wave of AI cryptoart — among the most acclaimed releases of 2022. Its
landscape is based on the sightseers who watched the 1950s nuclear bomb tests in
the desert outside Las Vegas.

***Sightseers: Perimeter Town*** is a boutique set of 250 made in the months
after, drawn from the glitched and morphic wastelands on the perimeter of that
world. Described as a Norman Harman AI collaboration.

**The release had an auction attached.** The artist-curated **Ground: Zero**
auction of 25 pieces opened *Perimeter Town* on 2 June 2023, one day before the
collection itself. The auction page carries the same contract, so those 25 are
not a separate edition.

## Provenance

**Francisco Carolinum, Linz — permanent collection.** *SIGHTSEERS 179* is one of
eleven works in the Sovrn Full Ethereum Set. Curator Julia Staudach; OÖ
Landes-Kultur GmbH. <https://www.sovrn.art/collections/fransisco-carolinum>

**Arab Bank (Switzerland) Ltd — Art by ABS collection.** Harman holds an artist
entry in the bank's collection. *This is not documented on sovrn.art* — the
source is the bank's own page, and it is also the independent source for
*SIGHTSEERS* being 500 works, 1/1 of 500:
<https://www.arabbank.ch/about-us/art/collection/artist/norman-harman/>

*Perimeter Town* is **not** in the Francisco Carolinum set — that set is eleven
works and does not cover every Ethereum collection.

## One handling note

title.** It drops the colon and the word order shifts:

    https://www.raster.art/artwork/sightseers-perimeter-town-by-norman-harman

and *SIGHTSEERS* is the plain one:
