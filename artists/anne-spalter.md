## The artist

A recognised pioneer of digital art. Spalter founded the first digital fine arts
courses at **Brown** and **RISD** in the 1990s and wrote *The Computer in the
Visual Arts*. With Michael Spalter she curates **Spalter Digital**, one of the
world's largest private collections of early computer art.

Her work is held by the **Centre Pompidou**, the **V&A**, the **RISD Museum** and
the **Buffalo AKG**, and has been shown at Sotheby's and Phillips. Featured in
*DIGITAL ART: 20 Pioneers* (Schiffer, 2025), *On NFTs* (Taschen, 2025) and
*Dismantling the Patriarchy, Bit by Bit* (Bloomsbury, 2021).

- <https://annespalter.com>

***AI Spaceships* was the first Sovrn Curated release.** Worth saying — it opens
the programme.

Climate change has made Earth uninhabitable. The last people build spaceships and
try to leave; some fail at launch, some warp to the past and meet steampunk
battleships, some warp forward into highly evolved and deadly plants. Research on
the plants reveals a way onward: the ships use plant extracts to open neon
hyperspace tunnels to a distant Earth future, landing in the frozen north, the
tropical equator and the desert.

*RABBIT TAKEOVER*: a lone rabbit cavorts through a post-armageddon world of
abandoned factories and trans-dimensional portals, presiding over mayhem, with
evidence of the rabbit's role surfacing across history.

## Provenance

**Francisco Carolinum, Linz — permanent collection.** *Outerspace Armada 15/501*
from *AI Spaceships* is one of eleven works in the Sovrn Full Ethereum Set,
accepted into the museum's permanent collection of photography and media art.
Facilitated by **Julia Staudach**, Curator for Digital Art; institution is OÖ
Landes-Kultur GmbH. <https://www.sovrn.art/museums/fransisco-carolinum>

That title also independently corroborates the edition size: the museum's own
listing reads "15/501".

*RABBIT TAKEOVER* is **not** in the Francisco Carolinum set. The set is eleven
works and does not cover every Ethereum collection, despite sometimes being
described as complete.

## Two handling notes

**1. The Raster slug for *AI Spaceships* is irregular.** It carries the artist's
name twice:

    https://www.raster.art/artwork/anne-spalter-ai-spaceships-by-anne-spalter

Every other Sovrn collection follows `<title>-by-<artist>`. We link the form
above because it is what resolves; if you normalise it at some point, tell us and
we will follow.

**2. *RABBIT TAKEOVER*'s release date appears nowhere on sovrn.art.** The date of
19 December 2022 comes from the artist's own submission corpus, not from our
site. It is sound, but if you need to cite a public source for it, ask us and we
will point you at the document rather than have you cite a page that does not
carry it.
