# Mikey Woodbridge — reference notes

Compiled from sovrn.art, which published *Latent Couture*. Companion to
**raster.art/artist/mikey-woodbridge/about**.

There is a display note at the end that matters more here than on any other
Sovrn collection. Please read it before generating thumbnails.

---

## The artist

Australian-born singer, painter, performer, fashion icon and digital artist.
Collaborations include **Rihanna** (designer), **Sink the Pink** (performer),
**Nick Knight** (model), **Adidas** (ambassador), and **Anton Corbijn & Depeche
Mode** (film).

In their own words, the blockchain work "uses tokens to translate the electric
currents of physical presence into the cultural currency of digital presence."

- <https://mikeywoodbridge.com>

555 AI fashion statements generated from photographs of Woodbridge's own years as
a performer, painter, designer and nightlife icon. Their framing is worth
quoting: "the opposite of a deepfake, this mode of generative AI reaches into the
latent space" — where a look is a meme, each of these statements says *this
person is possible*.

## The most exhibited Sovrn collection

*Latent Couture* is the most institutionally exhibited work in the programme.

**KINDL — Zentrum für zeitgenössische Kunst, Berlin.** *POLY. A Fluid Show*,
Maschinenhaus M2, 17 September 2023 – 25 February 2024, curated by **Solvej
Helweg Ovesen**. Woodbridge showed the **full collection as a wall-scale printed
grid** and performed at both the opening and the closing. Publication by Distanz,
ed. Solvej Helweg Ovesen and Kathrin Becker.
<https://www.sovrn.art/exhibitions/kindl>

**Vitra Design Museum, Vitra Schaudepot, Weil am Rhein.** *Science Fiction
Design: From Space Age to Metaverse*, 18 May 2024 – 10 May 2026 — **twenty-one
works shown for two years**. Curated by Susanne Graner and Nina Steinmüller,
creative direction by Andrés Reisinger. <https://www.sovrn.art/exhibitions/vitra>

**Francisco Carolinum, Linz — permanent collection.** *I enrage them.* is one of
eleven works in the Sovrn Full Ethereum Set. Curator Julia Staudach.
<https://www.sovrn.art/collections/fransisco-carolinum>

Photography credit for KINDL images is **Jens Ziehe**, though the credit is
unconfirmed for some of the images in circulation — worth checking with us before
you publish one.

## Display note: do not centre-crop these

If you crop *Latent Couture* to a square thumbnail from the centre, **you cut the
headpieces off.** The compositions put their subject high in the frame and the
headpiece is frequently the whole point of the look.

Our own tooling makes cropping opt-in for this reason, and when it does crop it
trims from the **top**, never the centre. If your card layout needs a fixed
aspect ratio, letterbox rather than crop, or crop from the bottom.

This is the one collection in the programme where a default centre-crop
materially damages the work rather than merely tightening it.
