Compiled from sovrn.art, which published *cope. Vol 1*. Companion to
**raster.art/artist/alex-headlam** — note that `/artist/aleqth/about` redirects
there, so your canonical filing is under the given name.

---

## The artist

Alex Headlam, who works as **aleqth**. He began with play-doh cars, moved to
paper and to automotive and vampiric themes, and started sharing work online at
**15**.

The "Cope" moniker arrived in **2020**, and stands for making the best of any
situation and seeing the world from a reinvigorated perspective.

**Dean Baquet**, then chief editor of *The New York Times*, bought three of his
drawings.

A **book**, made and self-published in **October 2021** in an edition of 50
hardcover copies, whose pages were drawn day by day with the process shared live
on social media. Every page, plus the front and back covers, is in the
collection.

That is worth being precise about, because it changes what the edition *is*: this
is not a series of 201 independent images but a single book, tokenised leaf by
leaf. Read in order it is the object it came from.

The count is `totalSupply()` read from the contract.
